Common questions

Is my progress saved?
Yes. Your best scores, coins, cars and levels are saved on your device and are kept when the game is updated. Deleting the app removes them.

How do I unlock more levels and cars?
Levels open when you reach a score target, or you can open them with coins you collect in the game. Cars are bought with coins. There are no in-app purchases.

How does tilt steering work?
Turn on "Gyro steering" in Settings, then tilt the phone left or right to change lane.

The game runs slowly.
Set Graphics to "Low" in Settings.

Leaderboards do not show.
Leaderboards use Apple Game Center. Sign in to Game Center in the iPhone Settings app.
