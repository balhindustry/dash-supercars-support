Privacy Policy

Dash Supercars 1 and Dash Supercars 2 · Last updated 19 November 2026

This policy explains how the Dash Supercars games ("the games") published by BALH Group Oy (Business ID 3642781-5, Helsinki, Finland) handle information.

We do not collect personal data

The games do not collect, store or send any personal data to us or to third parties. The games work offline. They contain no advertising, no analytics and no tracking tools, and they have no user accounts.

Data stored on your device

Your game progress (scores, coins, unlocked cars and levels, and settings) is stored only on your own device. We cannot see it. It is removed when you delete the app.

Game Center

If you are signed in to Apple Game Center, the games send your scores and achievements to Game Center so that they can appear on leaderboards. Game Center is provided by Apple and is governed by Apple's own privacy policy. You can use the games without Game Center.

Motion sensor

If you turn on tilt steering, the games read the phone's motion sensor to steer the car. This information is used only on your device while you play and is not stored or sent anywhere.

Children

The games do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
